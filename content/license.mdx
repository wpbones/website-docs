in personal and commercial projects.

The LGPL-3.0 is written as a set of additional permissions on top of the GNU General Public License
version 3, so the repository ships both texts: [`LICENSE`](https://github.com/wpbones/WPBones/blob/master/LICENSE)
(LGPL-3.0) and [`COPYING`](https://github.com/wpbones/WPBones/blob/master/COPYING) (GPL-3.0).

## The boilerplates

The **WP Kirk boilerplates** — the fourteen `WPKirk-*-Boilerplate` plugins — are released under the
[MIT license](https://opensource.org/licenses/MIT). They are templates: copy one to start your own
plugin and license the result as you choose, commercial plugins included.

A plugin published in the WordPress.org directory still needs a GPL-compatible license. MIT is one;
most plugins declare `GPLv2 or later`, as the [submission checklist](/docs/getting-started/before-submit-to-wordpress-repository)
shows.
