# Privacy policy

_Last updated: 5 October 2026_

WP Bones is a free, open-source framework, and this site is its documentation. It has no accounts and no forms. What it does process is listed here, including what the services it loads can see.

## Who is responsible

The data controller is **{config.legal.owner}** ({config.legal.brand}), an independent software developer in Italy, VAT number {`IT${config.legal.vatNumber}`}. Write to [hello@undolog.com](mailto:hello@undolog.com) about anything on this page. See also the [Legal notice](/docs/legal).

## What this site processes

- **Visit statistics.** Visits are counted by privacy-friendly analytics that use no cookies and build no profile of you: pages viewed, the referring site, country, and browser or device type, in aggregate. Visits are grouped by a hash of the request that is discarded after 24 hours.
- **Server logs.** The hosting provider that serves these pages processes your IP address and the details of each request to deliver the site and keep it secure; the request logs we can see are kept for one day.
- **The chat widget.** A contact widget (OpenWidget, by Text, Inc.) is loaded on every page through Google Tag Manager. Loading it sends your IP address and browser details to Google and to the widget's provider, and the provider sets cookies on its own domain (`livechatinc.com`) that identify your browser for up to 13 months. If you write in the chat, your messages and anything you type in it go to the provider and to us.
- **Fonts.** The fonts are loaded from Google Fonts, so Google sees your IP address.
- **Pictures from other services.** The Product Hunt badge is loaded from Product Hunt, which sets a short-lived security cookie, and some screenshots in the documentation are loaded from GitHub. Both see your IP address.
- **Your browser's local storage** remembers, in your browser only, your colour scheme and that you closed the release banner. None of it is sent to us.

## Links to other services

## Why, and your rights

We process the personal data described here to run and protect the site, to understand which pages help, and to answer you when you write: our legitimate interest, or the service you asked for. The hosting, analytics, font and widget providers may process it outside the European Union, including in the United States: the providers rely on the EU–US Data Privacy Framework or on the European Commission's standard contractual clauses, and we will tell you which on request.

Under the GDPR you can ask to access, correct or delete your personal data, to restrict or object to its processing, and to receive it in a portable format: write to [hello@undolog.com](mailto:hello@undolog.com). You can also lodge a complaint with the Italian data protection authority, the [Garante per la protezione dei dati personali](https://www.garanteprivacy.it/).
